SLIDE 01
Business Idea and Introduction

💡 What we offer

A direct-to-consumer online craft marketplace connecting certified rural Gujarat artisans (Jamnagar Bandhani, Patan Patola, Dhamadka Ajrakh, Gundiyali Terracotta) with conscious buyers.

🎯 Main purpose

Dismantle exploitative middleman cartels so rural handloom weavers retain 90–92% of retail price, preserving 5,000 years of living cultural heritage.

⭐ What makes it different

Dual-role platform (distinct Buyer & Artisan Seller portals), live price and stock customizer, DPDP Act 2023 legal consent, and multi-threaded concurrency reservation.

SLIDE 02
Problem Identification and Idea Generation

⚠️ The problem

  • Middlemen extract 50–60% margins
  • Artisans earn below daily living wage
  • Powerloom knockoffs flood retail markets
  • Buyers lack verifiable GI provenance

👥 Who faces it

  • 200,000+ weavers across Gujarat
  • Urban conscious handloom buyers
  • Handicraft preservation societies
  • Institutional & festive gift buyers

💡 How the idea came

  • Field observations in Bhujodi & Patan
  • Massive gap between loom cost & retail price
  • GI Registry laws lacked e-commerce backing
  • Need for automated cloud fair trade tech
🎯 Jobs-to-be-Done: "When I purchase authentic Indian heritage crafts, I want certified provenance and direct artisan payout, so my money empowers rural families rather than middlemen."

Java Core and OOP Architecture

Classes & Inheritance

Polymorphism & Interfaces

PaymentMethod interface implemented by UPI, Card, and Wallet test payment classes.

Custom Exceptions

OutOfStockException and PaymentFailedException for bulletproof error recovery.

ExecutorService Concurrency

Multi-threaded worker pool with ReentrantLock guarantees zero overselling during spikes.

BufferedWriter File I/O

Generates downloadable catalog CSVs and human-readable order tax invoice receipts.

Spring Boot 3 + REST

Clean Controller-Service-Repository multi-module architecture with Actuator monitoring.
